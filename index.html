Scoring Patterns

From Artistic Scoring for Beautiful Sourdough Bread Designs by The Sourdough Mama — 4 signature designs and 11 core techniques.

Signature Patterns

HIBISCUS SOURDOUGH

A five-petal flower scored around a string-marked center. Light interior vein cuts and deeper petal outlines separate into full blooming petals with a 3D scoring bake.

BUTTERFLY SOURDOUGH

Symmetrical wings scored on either side of a center string. Feather-light interior veins pair with deeper outer-edge cuts that lift the wings apart as the loaf bakes.

SOURDOUGH BOW BREAD

A bow tied with guide strings: light cuts shape the knot and ribbon folds, while deeper cuts define the loops and tails so they lift apart into a dimensional bow.

FAIRY PUMPKINS

An eight-point pattern scored around crossed guide strings, with light veins inside each lobe, deeper outlines between them, and skewer-poked dots scattered around the edges.

Core Techniques

FINE LINES

Let the blade barely graze the surface with a light, steady glide — the lighter the touch, the finer the line; more pressure widens the cut.

WHEAT STALKS

Trace a center stem, then add quick strokes on alternating sides that lean toward it without touching. Start each new stroke halfway up the last, and finish with one straight tip cut.

DOTS

Poke the floured surface with a toothpick or skewer, just enough to break it. A quick way to add texture or fill negative space between larger cuts.

FLOWERS

Work from the center outward — a straw can mark a round center, and floss laid across the dough helps guide even petal placement. Try pointed, rounded, or jagged petal edges.

ROSES

Start with a small ‘u’ in the center, then build small wavy ‘c’ cuts just outside it on alternating sides. Keep building outward; deeper cuts open into fuller petals.

LEAVES

Cut the outline first with a fine line, score a center stem, then add veins as short 45° cuts off the stem. Snip the edges with small scissors for texture.

FIVE MINUTE EXPANSION SCORE

Bake covered for 5 minutes so the surface firms up, then make the deep expansion cut. It holds its shape better than scoring cold dough and lets the loaf ‘breathe’ before it finishes baking.

EXPANSION SCORE CARVING

After a five-minute expansion score, go back in with scissors and a lame to trim the newly exposed edges into shapes — a great base for roses or other add-ons.

SCALLOPED EXPANSION SCORE

Cut curved, scalloped edges instead of straight ones during the five-minute expansion score, when the crust has firmed up. A jar lid makes a handy curve guide.

CROSSCUT EXPANSION SCORE

Best for round loaves. Stencil a line across the center, turn 180° and stencil a second line to quarter it, then cut from each end just to the center — never straight through — to avoid snagging.

3D SCORING

Score the design lightly, bake covered for 5 minutes, then cut deep and gently lift the pieces up and away with a lame and skewer. Parchment balls underneath hold the lifted flaps apart as it finishes baking.

How to use

A simple guide for this bake timer.

1. Choose a recipe

Tap Select Recipe and pick Sourdough Bread, Pizza, Focaccia, Cioccolatella, or a custom recipe. Built-in recipes load ingredients and bake settings for you. Use Qty to scale the batch.

2. Work through the phases

Tap a phase (Levain, Autolyse, Bulk, and so on) to open it. There's no running clock — when you finish that step in real life, tap Mark Done and it logs the time for you. Tap it again to un-mark it if you tapped by mistake. Use Skip this step if you do not need it.

Each Done note shows how much time passed since the previous step, calculated automatically from the timestamps — no need to watch a clock.

3. Folds

Open Folds during bulk. Fill in Start time and End time for the rest period (about 30 minutes), then tap Log Stretch & Folds or Log Coil Folds. It's added to the log below, and the End time automatically becomes the Start time for your next fold. For Cioccolatella, add chocolate on folds 2–3 when reminded. Close the Folds panel yourself when you are done.

4. When a timer finishes

You get a sound, optional vibration, and a large bread screen. Tap the bread to dismiss. For most steps that also closes the phase; Folds and Covered Bake stay open so you can continue.

5. Flour used, Temp & PH

On Levain, Autolyse, and Bulk you can type which flour you used, plus Temp and PH for that stage. On Levain, you can also note which starter you used. Pre-Shape, Bench Rest, and Cold Proof each have their own Temp and PH fields too. All of these notes are saved with the bake.

6. Progress & chart

Completed steps appear under Progress. A vertical bar chart shows how long each step took.

7. Bake

Bread-style bakes use Score, then Covered Bake and Uncovered Bake. With a Dutch oven, Covered is lid on; Uncovered is lid off. Pizza and Focaccia use one timed bake with their preset temperature and minutes.

8. End the session

Tap End Session. Review the recap, then Save to Bake Log or Discard this bake.

9. Bake Log & Save

BAKE LOG shows past bakes. SAVE backs up the app, exports data, shares a read-only copy, or enables notifications.

10. Extra tools

Loaf / Pizza / Focaccia weight and Baker’s % help scale and check hydration. Export/Import recipes backs up custom recipes. Closing mid-bake may offer Resume next time.
